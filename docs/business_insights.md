# Business Insights

## Sales

- West region generated the highest sales.
- Technology is the highest revenue category.
- Consumer segment contributes the largest share of sales.

## Profit

- Technology generated the highest profit.
- Some products consistently generate losses.
- Higher discounts often reduce profitability.

## Recommendation

- Increase focus on Technology products.
- Review pricing strategy for loss-making products.
- Reduce excessive discounts on low-margin items.
